SERVICE DESIGN • GOVTECH • 2026 UX INDIA BEST INNOVATION

Simplifying the grueling administrative maze of death for grieving families.

When a family member passes away, surviving kin must navigate up to 14 disconnected government and financial entities during acute grief. Prayan transforms bureaucratic fragmentation into an empathetic, single-touchpoint transition service—awarded the Best Student Innovation Award at UX India 2026.

Role Service Designer & Qualitative Lead
Team 3 Designers (MIT ID Graduation Project)
Timeline 4 Months (Field Immersion & Co-Design)
Accolade Gold • UX India 2026
Field Insights

Bureaucracy does not stop for grief.

Acute bereavement causes physiological memory impairment, extreme fatigue, and executive dysfunction. Yet Indian bureaucracy demands that surviving family members immediately visit municipal corporations, multiple private banks, life insurers, and utility boards.

Every institution demands identical certified copies, notarized heirship affidavits, and physical appearances. One minor spelling discrepancy on an Aadhaar card can freeze bank pensions for 9+ months.

SURVIVOR TESTIMONIAL

"I was standing in a bank line on day 4 after losing my father, clutching 12 paper folders while clerks argued about gazette seals. We didn't even have time to weep."

SERVICE ARCHITECTURE

A unified, grief-paced public service system.

Prayan introduces a "single statutory dispatch"—grieving families submit their verified identity once, triggering an automated legal grace period and asynchronous institutional clearance.

PRAYAN SERVICE PROTOCOL SPECIFICATION

Grief-First Milestone Framework

PRAYAN V3.2 • DEPLOYED SPEC
PHASE 01: DAYS 1—7
Statutory Grace Freeze

Instant dispatch of death registration from crematorium/hospital. Automatic 30-day statutory freeze on loan penalty debits and aggressive bank summons.

PHASE 02: DAYS 8—30
The Digital Dossier

Surviving kin fill a single guided conversational intake. Prayan compiles verified documents into a pre-filled, QR-stamped universal packet.

PHASE 03: DAYS 31+
Asynchronous Clearance

A single certified notary officer reviews the packet at home. All 14 institutions receive authenticated DigiLocker tokens without family visits.

INTERACTION MICRO-COPY & TONE

From cold penal bureaucracy to respectful companionship.

Traditional portals use hostile legal language ("Failure to furnish succession certificate within 30 days will result in forfeiture"). We rewrote every single interaction to provide clarity, comfort, and cognitive breathing room.

EXISTING MUNICIPAL / BANK PORTALS PUNITIVE
NOTICE: Deceased account holder records flagged. Nominee must submit physical Affidavit Annexure 4-B within 14 days or interest accumulation will cease.

Requires users to comprehend complex penal clauses during peak emotional grief, forcing frantic trips to lawyers.

PRAYAN COMPASSIONATE INTERACTION GRIEF-INFORMED
We have paused account debits for you. Take all the time your family needs. When you are ready, here is the one document required to transfer savings to the designated nominee.

Provides reassurance, protects from predatory financial penalties, and clearly outlines simple, stress-free next steps.

CANDID REFLECTIONS • FIELD ETHICS

Designing in the shadow of grief taught me humility.

Conducting qualitative interviews with people who had recently lost parents or spouses was the most emotionally demanding research I have ever led. Standard UX testing scripts feel absurd and callous when someone is breaking down in front of you.

We had to build rigorous trauma-informed ethical protocols: allowing participants to stop at any second, conducting interviews in neutral comforting spaces, and ensuring our presence felt supportive rather than extractive.

Winning the Best Student Innovation Award at UX India 2026 was humbling, but the real victory was having municipal registrars and banking ombudsmen review our system and say: "This is how civic service in India should have worked all along."
