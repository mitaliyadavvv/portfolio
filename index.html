[ BIO ]

I believe that great design is neither decorative nor sterile—it is the deliberate alignment of complex technical systems with the grain of human nature.

My work spans the entire spectrum: from qualitative field immersion with frontline workers in noisy factories to architecting design tokens and writing code prototypes. Having designed at Siemens Digital Industries Software and Godrej & Boyce, I thrive when tackling high-density, mission-critical problem spaces.

Beyond software, I serve as the Student President of the Association of Designers of India (ADI) at MIT Institute of Design, Pune, fostering a community of practice around ethical tech, inclusive services, and craft.
