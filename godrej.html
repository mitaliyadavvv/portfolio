GODREJ & BOYCE • FIELD ENTERPRISE MOBILITY • 2024

Cutting CRM lead friction by 52% for 2,400+ industrial dealer reps.

Industrial sales representatives for Godrej & Boyce operate in noisy fabrication plants and remote warehouses where connectivity is volatile. The legacy enterprise mobile app suffered from an unusable 9-step form that led to 68% field abandonment. We redesigned the mobile experience with one-thumb ergonomics, smart defaults, and guaranteed offline queueing.

Role Mobile Product Design Intern
Team 1 Design Lead, 2 Android Eng, 1 PM
Timeline 3 Months (Research to Pilot Rollout)
Key Metric 52% Fewer Taps • 15h Saved / Mo
Field Reality

The ergonomic constraints of the factory floor.

Software designers often work comfortably on 32-inch monitors in quiet air-conditioned studios. But an industrial sales representative lives in high decibels, glaring midday sunlight, and dusty environments.

Field reps routinely hold a heavy product catalogue or measuring tape in one hand, forcing all smartphone operations to be executed with a single thumb.

SHADOWING DISCOVERY

Reps bypassed the corporate CRM app completely during client meetings. They scribbled notes on torn paper napkins, then spent 3 hours every Sunday night manually back-filling fake data into the system just to satisfy management quotas.

INPUT AUDIT: LEGACY VS REDESIGN
BEFORE REDESIGN
21 Inputs

Nested cascading dropdowns, mandatory postal pin codes, multi-screen navigation, zero offline saving.

AFTER REDESIGN
3 Taps

Geo-tagged customer match, quick audio voice memo, and card OCR scanner with instant offline queue.

DEALER ADOPTION
94%

Daily active usage rate reached within 4 weeks of pilot deployment across the Western India division.

ADMIN TIME SAVED
15 hrs

Average monthly hours saved per representative by eliminating weekend retrospective data entry.

MOBILE INTERACTION DESIGN

Engineered for one-thumb speed and industrial sunlight.

Every primary action was moved to the bottom 40% of the screen. We engineered an offline-first state machine that queues entries locally and syncs silently when connectivity is restored.

INTERACTION COMPARISON

Tearing down the barrier to field entry.

When an enterprise interface requires 21 mandatory fields before allowing a save, users don't become more thorough—they rebel by entering nonsensical dummy text.

BEFORE: DESKTOP FORM SQUEEZED TO MOBILE 68% ABANDONMENT
  • 21 individual text and numerical fields.
  • Modal date-pickers that clashed with system keyboards.
  • Network dropouts caused all unsaved form inputs to be wiped out.
  • Sales reps were forced to return to desktops to complete leads.
AFTER: TACTILE MOBILE-FIRST LOGGING 94% ADOPTION
  • 3 quick taps to capture an active prospect.
  • GPS-based client lookup eliminates typing company names.
  • Instant offline queueing with optimistic UI updates.
  • Quick voice memos auto-transcribe into structured specifications.
CANDID REFLECTIONS • STAKEHOLDER NEGOTIATIONS

Persuading leadership that fewer inputs mean better data.

The hardest challenge in this project was not the UI layout—it was persuading senior sales executives that removing 18 mandatory fields would actually increase their data accuracy.

When we presented our audit showing that 73% of the "mandatory" postal codes and product sub-codes in the existing database were filled with fake keystrokes like "NA" and "000000", leadership finally understood: mandatory forms create compliance theater, not truth.

By respecting the dealer reps’ time and physical conditions, we transformed the mobile CRM from a dreaded surveillance chore into an empowering daily companion.
