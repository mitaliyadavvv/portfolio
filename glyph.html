Turning 10,000+ scattered enterprise icons into instant conversational discovery.

01. THE PROBLEM SPACE

The vocabulary gap between design and code.

In industrial automation software, a simple component like a "shutoff valve" has 18 distinct engineering variations depending on whether it’s hydraulic, pneumatic, motorized, or fail-safe.

Designers searched visually using physical metaphors ("emergency red handle"), while frontend engineers searched by internal token taxonomy ($sys-actuator-emergency-24).

CRITICAL AUDIT INSIGHT

42% of icon searches failed because creators were searching for intent and purpose ("indicates sensor offline") rather than literal taxonomy.

CANDID REFLECTION

Designing for trust in enterprise generative tooling.

When we first launched the alpha version, users were hesitant because AI search results felt opaque—they didn’t know why an icon had been surfaced or whether it adhered to Siemens’ strict accessibility standards.

We solved this not with more LLM cleverness, but with radical transparency: every recommendation showed its cosine similarity score, token lineage, and direct Figma library link. Trust returned the moment users felt the system wasn't "guessing," but merely accelerating their own judgment.

The project taught me that the real art of AI product design isn't generating answers—it's building the guardrails, ergonomics, and latency tolerances that make autonomous tools reliable in mission-critical environments.
